How it decides

It tries thousands of arrangements of your parts and keeps the one that reaches the goal with the most Δv left over. Rockets are scored stage by stage with the rocket equation: the climb off Kerbin is flown in a 2D simulation with drag (MechJeb's classic ascent profile, drag from frontal area and a Mach curve as in Project-Atmospheric-Drag), and the rest of the trip uses the stock Δv map (then transfer, capture, landing and return), with sea-level Isp low in the atmosphere and minimum thrust-to-weight for each part of the trip (1.25 off the pad, 2.0 for landings). Spaceplanes are flown in a 2D simulation: runway takeoff, jet climb with intake air limits, rocket ascent to 80 km, circularisation. Part figures are from the KSP wiki; a few are estimates and are marked.

Pick your parts, choose a goal, then run.
