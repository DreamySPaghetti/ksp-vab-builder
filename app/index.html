How it decides

It tries thousands of arrangements of your parts and keeps the one that reaches the goal with the most Δv left over. Rockets are scored stage by stage with the rocket equation against the stock Δv map (3,400 m/s to low Kerbin orbit, then transfer, capture, landing and return), with sea-level Isp low in the atmosphere and minimum thrust-to-weight for each part of the trip (1.25 off the pad, 2.0 for landings). Spaceplanes are flown in a 2D simulation: runway takeoff, jet climb with intake air limits, rocket ascent to 80 km, circularisation. Part figures are from the KSP wiki; a few are estimates and are marked.

Pick your parts, choose a goal, then run.
